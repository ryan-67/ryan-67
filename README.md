## About

I build data-heavy products end to end — pipelines on AWS, warehouses and marts, React/TypeScript frontends, and grounded AI analysts.

Most recently I shipped **[nucky](https://nucky.gg)** (repo: [`lol-dashboard`](https://github.com/ryan-67/lol-dashboard)): a production LoL esports analytics SaaS with automated ETL, ML foresight models, and a subscription chat analyst.

Previously at **Pepperdata**, I owned daily ETL/ELT on EMR/EKS/S3 for an enterprise Kubernetes optimization platform used by large financial and consumer clients.
